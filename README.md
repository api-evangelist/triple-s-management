Triple-S Management Corporation is the largest managed care company in Puerto Rico in terms of membership, providing health, life, and property and casualty insurance. A subsidiary of GuideWell (parent of Florida Blue), Triple-S operates as an independent licensee of the Blue Cross Blue Shield Association for Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands.

**Website:** [https://management.grupotriples.com/en/](https://management.grupotriples.com/en/)

---

## Subsidiaries

- **Triple-S Salud** — Managed care health insurance
- **Triple-S Advantage** — Medicare Advantage plans
- **Triple-S Vida** — Life insurance
- **Triple-S Propiedad** — Property and casualty insurance
- **Salus** — Medical clinics and telemedicine
- **CarePoint** — Independent Practice Association (IPA)

## Provider Resources

### Triple-S Salud Provider Portal
The Mi Triple-S provider portal gives healthcare providers access to insured eligibility verification, claims and payments management, re-credentialing, and the provider directory. Supports EDI 837 electronic claims submission.

- **Provider Portal:** [https://service.ssspr.com/provider/login.aspx](https://service.ssspr.com/provider/login.aspx)
